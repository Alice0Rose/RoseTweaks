[h1]Features[/h1]
This mod reworks the Stellar Cannon from a lackluster waste of alloys to the galactic super-weapon it was always meant to be. It also indirectly reduces the amount the AI spams the Stellar Cannon due to it requiring a higher tier tech that is locked behind the Colossus Project AP (or Galactic Wonders AP if you don't have the Apocalypse DLC).
