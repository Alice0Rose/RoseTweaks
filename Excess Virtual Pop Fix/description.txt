[h1]Features[/h1]
This mod fixes the issue of wayyyy too many virtual pops spawning on your colonies (especially noticeable with arkships after v4.4) by "ethically" removing all the extra pops via a yearly pulse event and whenever colony infrastructure is changed :)

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
This mod overrides the cybernetics.2026 and cybernetics.2027 events in machine_age_events_2.txt, which handles creating and destroying virtual pops, so its unlikely to have many compatibility issues with other mods.
