[h1]Features[/h1]
This mod reimplements the policy for Eternal Vigilance which automatically created defense platforms for free every 6 months which was added in version 4.0 of Stellaris and then removed in version 4.3, with changes to make the feature more balanced and have better quality of life.

Instead of the single policy that was in the base game, this mod adds 2 policies: Coverage and Density. Coverage controls which starbases will automatically build defense platforms, and can range from just Bastion starbases to all starbases including outposts. Density, on the other hand, controls the percent of the defense platform cap that will be automatically built. It starts at 25%, with options for 50% and 75% locked behind the Starhold and Star Fortress technologies respectively. 

The main way this mod balances this powerful feature is with a scaling alloy cost that can be seen as an empire modifier. This scales off the Density policy, with the higher percentages incurring more alloy cost, and the number of starbases covered. This means that if you want maximum density on all of your starbases and outposts, its gonna take a pretty stronk economy.

The other way this mod balances out the feature is by despawning auto-built starbases if the density policy is brought down from a higher level to a lower level. This means you can't set it to 75%, let all the defense platforms build for free, and then set it back to 25% for the lower alloy cost. On the plus side, the implementation of this balancing feature also allows auto-built defense platforms to upgrade themselves automatically once you're at the auto-build cap.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
This mod only overrides the Eternal Vigilance ascension perk, so it should be compatible with most other mods :)

common\ascension_perks\00_ascension_perks.txt
- ap_eternal_vigilance
